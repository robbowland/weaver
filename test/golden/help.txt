KEYS
  ⏎  1-9          jump to the thread (1-9: that row)
  j k  g G        move; first, last
  /               search (esc: back)
  n               new thread (wvr new)
  x               close the thread, its nvim and its window
                  (and offers to remove a merged worktree)
  a               adopt: an agent you started by hand becomes a thread;
                  elsewhere, pick a conversation to resume as one
  r               rename the thread (empty: its agent's title)
  R               restart its agent, resuming its conversation (after an update)
  v  e  ctrl+e    jump and open its nvim (alt+v anywhere)
  s               the card ↔ the agent's screen
  tab  shift+tab  the next, previous view (THREADS, GITHUB, LINEAR)
  ctrl+r          reload
  ?               this help (? again: back)
  q  esc          back to your thread
  ctrl+q          quit the session, every agent and nvim (the threads
                  come back as wvr starts; mid-turn, it asks first)

THREADS
  [!]  needs you    [x]  done, not yet seen    [/]  working
  [x]  done, seen   [ ]  idle    [-]  interrupted

  ×2  two agents share the worktree: their turn diffs mix

LOST (its session went: a crash, or quit)
  ⏎               resume it in a new window (wvr resume)
  x               forget it (its worktree goes once nothing in it is unsaved)

ANYWHERE
  alt+y           in a thread: the board over it (tab: the views);
                  on home: each view, then back where you were
  alt+N           new thread (in a view: from the item)
  alt+1           the thread that has waited on you longest (row 1)
  alt+v  alt+V    the thread's nvim; full screen
  alt+j  alt+k    next, previous window (past the views)
  alt+h  alt+l    the pane left, right

MOUSE
  click           a row; a key shown, as if pressed
  click twice     the row, as ⏎
  right click     a menu of what can be done there
  wheel           scroll (a pane: back through it)
  status line     THREADS, a view or a count: go there
